# Organica
## Project Brief

### 1. Project Overview

Organica is a software system for managing and organizing personal activities.

The project aims to provide an environment dedicated to planning, managing and monitoring daily activities, allowing users to maintain a clear overview of their responsibilities.

Organica seeks to combine simplicity, flexibility and clarity, avoiding an unnecessarily complex organizational structure.

### 2. Problem Statement

Managing personal activities can become difficult when commitments, deadlines and responsibilities are scattered across different tools or organized without a coherent structure.

Furthermore, some task management systems may be excessively complex for the needs of individual users, introducing features and organizational mechanisms that are not always necessary.

Organica aims to address these challenges through a system dedicated to personal task management, with particular attention to organizational clarity and ease of use.

### 3. Project Vision

The vision of Organica is to create a personal organization system that allows users to manage their activities in a simple, organized and flexible way.

The system should provide a structure capable of adapting to individual needs, without imposing a rigid or unnecessarily complex organizational model.

### 4. Project Objectives

The main objectives of Organica are:

- To facilitate the management of personal activities.
- To allow users to organize and monitor their responsibilities.
- To provide a clear overview of ongoing and completed activities.
- To support the prioritization of tasks and the management of deadlines.
- To offer a simple and consistent user experience.
- To apply software engineering principles through a structured process of analysis, specification and design.

### 5. Intended Users

Organica is primarily intended for individuals who want to organize their personal activities, manage commitments and maintain an overall view of their responsibilities.

The system is designed for individual use rather than collaborative project management.

### 6. Product Concept

Organica is conceived as a personal task management system characterized by a simple, clear and flexible approach to activity organization.

The project's visual direction is inspired by natural and organic elements, with the aim of creating a balanced and unobtrusive user experience.

The specific functionalities and system behaviors will be defined during the Requirements Engineering phase.

### 7. Project Scope

The project focuses on the analysis, specification and design of a software system for personal task management.

The scope includes requirements definition, domain modeling, software architecture design, software design and the definition of verification and validation strategies.

### 8. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a structured and documented software engineering project describing the design process of Organica, from problem definition to requirements specification, architectural design and quality verification planning.
